# Pantry

## What is Pantry?

Pantry is a website that helps people keep track of food at home. It shows what they have, what they should use soon, what they could cook, and what they may need to buy.

## What problem does it solve?

People often forget what food they already have. Food can get hidden in the fridge or cupboard and go past its use-by date. People may also buy food they already own or struggle to decide what to cook.

Pantry puts this information in one place. It aims to help people waste less food, avoid buying the same thing twice, and make meal planning easier.

## Who is it for?

Pantry is for anyone who wants a simple way to manage food at home. It can be used by one person or by a household sharing one pantry.

## What can people do in Pantry?

### Add and manage food

People can add food with its name, amount, unit, type, and optional use-by date. They can add a photo, or Pantry will show a food icon. People can search, filter by food type, and sort the list by date, name, or when an item was added.

Pantry shows date labels such as “Expires today,” “In 2 days,” or “Expired 1 day ago.” People can edit or delete items. Pantry asks before deleting and confirms when changes are saved.

Food types are Produce, Dairy, Pantry, Protein, Bakery, and Other. These types do not tell you the exact shelf or drawer where an item is kept.

### Find something to cook

People can ask Pantry for recipe ideas using the food they have. They can choose a food preference, such as Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, or Dairy-free. Pantry tries to use food that is nearing its use-by date.

People can open an idea to see its ingredients and steps. If an ingredient is missing, they can add it to the shopping list.

Recipe ideas are suggestions. People should check that each recipe is right for them. If recipe ideas are unavailable, Pantry tries to make simple ideas from the food in the pantry.

### Keep a shopping list

People can add items to the shopping list themselves or add missing ingredients from a recipe. Pantry skips names that are already in the pantry or on the list, even if the letters use different capitalisation. People can remove items when they no longer need them.

### Change preferences and get help

People can choose how soon Pantry should flag food that is nearing its use-by date. They can also choose a food preference for recipes. The Help button explains the main features.

## How people use Pantry

1. Add the food they have at home.
2. Check which items need to be used soon.
3. Look for a recipe idea.
4. Add missing ingredients to the shopping list.
5. Update or remove items as the pantry changes.

## Sign in

People can create an account with their name, email, and password, then sign in with the same email and password. Each account has its own pantry. The first account may also be prepared when Pantry is set up. Password reset and email verification are not available yet.

## What Pantry saves

Pantry items are saved with the pantry. Photos, shopping-list items, recipe ideas, and preferences are saved in the browser being used. They stay there after a refresh, but do not move to another browser or device.

## What Pantry does not do yet

- It does not scan food packages or find use-by dates automatically.
- It does not know when someone uses food. People must update or remove it themselves.
- A use-by date is only a reminder. Pantry cannot tell if food is safe to eat.
- Recipe ideas may not fit every allergy or dietary need. People should check the ingredients.
- Accounts have separate pantry items and browser-saved preferences. People cannot share a pantry across different accounts yet. Password reset and email verification are not available yet.
- The shopping list does not have checkboxes, prices, or item amounts. Bought items do not move into the pantry automatically.

## How we know Pantry is helping

Pantry is helping when people can quickly see what food they have, use food before they forget it, decide what to cook, and buy only what they need. It should make these things easier without making pantry upkeep feel like a chore.
